Recent maritime incidents such as the Nord Stream pipeline sabotage and Baltic Sea cable disruptions have highlighted the vulnerability of undersea infrastructure. Traditional survey methods are costly, slow to deploy, and limited by weather constraints. The proposed TUPER system addresses these challenges by enabling rapid, scalable, and cost-effective multi-AUV operations with minimal infrastructure and robust fault tolerance.
