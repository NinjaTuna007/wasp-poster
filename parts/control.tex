The control strategy is orchestrated through a behavior tree framework, allowing for modular and adaptive control. %The main strategy is as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item While cross-track deviation is low, focus on keeping up (fast and steady)
    \item When cross-track deviation increases, prioritize steering control (slow and furious)
    \item If turning, temporarily shift the setpoint against the direction of the turn
\end{itemize}
