\textbf{Why Semi-Circle?}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=0.05em]
    \item Optimal GDOP geometry
    \item 90° constraint for precision
    \item Scalable to 8+ followers
    \item Robust to leader loss
\end{itemize}
