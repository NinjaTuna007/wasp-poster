We deploy a team of AUVs in the field to conduct collaborative underwater surveys as follows:

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=wasppink!10, colframe=wasppink, boxrule=0.5pt]
% \textbf{Major Limitations:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1em, itemsep=0.05em]
    \item 2 Leaders with reliable positioning
    \item N Cheap followers with SONAR sensors
    \item Localization via acoustic ranging from the leaders (mobile acoustic beacons)
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}

After completing a survey, the AUVs return to the surface and transmit their collected data to a central processing unit for analysis. We then combine the overlapping data from multiple AUVs to create a 3D reconstruction of the underwater environment using neural rendering techniques.
